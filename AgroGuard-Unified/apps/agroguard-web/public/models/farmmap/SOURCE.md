# Farm map models and textures

Every file in this folder is generated by a script in `assets-src/farmmap/`. No third-party model, texture or photograph is included, so no outside licence applies. The project owns these files.

- Tool: Blender 5.2.2 LTS (GPL-3.0 software; its output is not covered by the GPL), glTF 2.0 exporter with Draco compression.
- `build_models.py`: rice clumps by growth stage, weed clumps, rice mill, pump, sluice gate, and the ground textures `mud_wet.jpg`, `soil_dry.jpg` and `bund_clay.jpg`.
- `build_scenery.py`: broadleaf tree, palm, conifer, bamboo, shrub, three rocks, reeds, banana plant, stilt farm hut, water buffalo and a low-poly cloud (about 70 m wide, scaled in the view by the cloud cover reading), each in two levels of detail, and the textures `water_normal.jpg`, `roof_tin.jpg`, `roof_tile.jpg` and `wall_plaster.jpg`. Full-detail plants, huts and animals carry ambient occlusion baked into their vertex colours with Cycles, so canopies and contact points read as shaded in the view without a runtime shadow pass.

Rebuild:

```
blender --background --factory-startup --python assets-src/farmmap/build_models.py -- public/models/farmmap
blender --background --factory-startup --python assets-src/farmmap/build_scenery.py -- public/models/farmmap
```

Rules:

- Shapes and colours are illustrative. They stand for a kind of plant, rock or structure. They are not a survey of what is at a place.
- Plant height, canopy cover and crop stage come from the season engines, not from these meshes.
- The scenery models carry one vertex colour set and one material, so each kind draws as one instanced mesh.
- Budgets are checked by `tests/farm-simulation/models.test.ts`: at most 300 KB per scenery file, and the triangle counts in `TRI_BUDGET` in `lib/fieldsim/models.ts`.
- The hut, banana plants, buffalo, palms, shrubs and reeds beside the main field are placed only on example farms, by `lib/farmview/homestead.ts`, and the view labels them illustrative. A traced farm never gets invented buildings or animals.
